What do these Status Flag / Peer Benchmark badges mean?

Status Flag

New — Launched within the selected age window — too early to judge performance yet.

Steady — Still selling at a reasonable pace relative to its own early performance, even if that pace was always modest.

Slowing — Recent selling pace has dropped below 70% of its early pace — an early warning sign.

Stagnant — Recent selling pace has collapsed below 30% of its early pace — this launch has lost real momentum, worth investigating (design relevance, pricing, or awareness).

Sold Out — Fully cleared — no remaining balance, no longer part of the inventory burden.

Early pace = sell-through in the first 3 months after launch. Recent pace = sell-through in the most recent 3 months relative to today.

Peer Benchmark

Peer Benchmark compares a cohort's sell-through so far against how similar product types in the same price range typically performed at the same age — helping catch underperforming launches early, before enough time has passed to show up as Slowing or Stagnant.

Above Peers — Selling at least 10 percentage points faster than comparable cohorts at this age.

On Par — Within 10 percentage points of comparable cohorts at this age.

Below Peers — Selling at least 10 percentage points slower than comparable cohorts at this age — worth a closer look even if Status Flag still reads New or Steady.

Insufficient Data — Fewer than 3 comparable peer cohorts (same Product Type + Price Range tier, old enough to have reached this cohort's own age) exist to benchmark against.

Level Size & Sell-Through Analysis

Product Type here is limited to structured types (Niche, Pedestal, Pet Niche, EBL, Baby Paradise) — flat-land types have no Level No to analyze.

What does Compensation Status mean?

For levels made physically larger to offset lower demand (e.g. positioned near the floor or ceiling), Insufficient means the size increase has not fully compensated — sell-through remains below what similarly-priced standard-size levels achieve. Sufficient means it has. Compensation Status only applies to levels flagged Enlarged (their most common Lot Dimension is physically larger than the product type's overall standard dimension); Standard-size levels show N/A.
